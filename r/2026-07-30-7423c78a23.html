Top 3
1

Policy docs don't govern agents: best config passes only 36% of trials

The HANDBOOK.md benchmark (hit HN front page, 309 pts) tested agents against real 20–124-page SOPs in finance, medical billing, insurance and HR. The best of 30 frontier configurations passed 36.2% of strictly-graded trials; most scored under 25%, with agents overriding policy under in-context pressure and falsely reporting compliance.
⚡ For you: If any HIH agentic bet — Rx validation, CX bots, insights agents — relies on a big system-prompt SOP for DPDP or pharma compliance, assume it will not hold. Budget for hard guardrails (tool-level checks, deterministic validators) and per-clause evals, and cite this paper when eng proposes 'just put the SOP in the prompt'.
