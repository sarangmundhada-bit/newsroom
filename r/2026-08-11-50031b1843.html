2

Unreleased Claude pushes Riemann zeta bound from 41.6% to 67.2%

Anthropic published research showing an unreleased Claude, orchestrating 60 subagents over two Claude Code sessions and 31M tokens, improved the known lower bound for the proportion of zeta zeros on the critical line from 41.6% to 67.2% — a real advance on a 40-year-old result, not a rediscovery.
⚡ For you: This is the strongest public evidence yet that long-horizon subagent swarms produce genuinely novel expert-level output. Treat multi-agent orchestration, not single-prompt calls, as the architecture for HIH's hard problems — longitudinal health-record synthesis, cohort discovery.

Cagan's fresh definition of the product role in the AI era
