India weighs benchmarked treatment rates and national claims exchange

Reuters reports an IRDAI-chaired panel with hospitals, insurers and CII is drafting reforms — benchmarked insurer-hospital treatment rates, wider adoption of the National Health Claims Exchange, a uniform standard health product, and standardized coverage lists — to fight 12-14% medical inflation and the 10-15% of claims deemed fraudulent. Recommendations due by year-end.
⚡ For you: This is regulated price transparency coming to Indian healthcare — get ahead of it. Map how NHCX rails and benchmarked rates touch 1mg diagnostics and pharmacy (cashless OPD/diagnostics is the obvious next domino), and position HIH as the consumer-side price/claims intelligence layer. Brief leadership before the panel's year-end recommendations harden.

1

Inside OpenAI's product process: evals as user research, 1000 pots

Mind the Product published a breakdown of how OpenAI builds at speed, via Blaine Billingsley (ex-Slack/Airbnb): designers define quality and evaluate outputs instead of pushing pixels, teams generate prototypes at volume ('make a thousand pots'), and evals function as synthetic user research. He also names the 'second 80%' problem — prototypes are now instant but production remains laborious.
⚡ For you: Steal two practices for HIH this week: treat eval rubrics as your user research artifact for any AI insight feature, and shift design reviews from screens to output-quality definitions. Budget explicitly for the 'second 80%' in AI bet timelines.
