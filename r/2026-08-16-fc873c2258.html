Top 3
1

OpenAI Foundation puts $100M behind AI-driven hepatitis C cure gap

OpenAI Foundation and the Common Health Coalition launched 'Breakthroughs to Follow-Through' — $100M to at least double hep C cure rates in 8 states within 2 years by using AI to find and reconnect diagnosed-but-untreated patients (only ~1 in 3 Americans diagnosed get treated within a year), then expand to HIV prevention and curable cancers.
⚡ For you: This is the exact HIH thesis validated at population scale: AI closing the diagnosis-to-treatment gap. Steal the playbook — use 1mg's diagnostics results plus pharmacy refill data to detect diagnosed-but-untreated chronic patients (diabetes, thyroid, lipids) and build an AI reconnect loop. Pitch it internally as India's version of Breakthroughs to Follow-Through.

Hippocratic AI ships agentic 'orchestrators' to run voice AI care teams

Hippocratic AI launched agentic orchestrators that coordinate teams of voice AI agents across patient-facing clinical workflows — a shift from single-purpose AI callers to managed multi-agent care operations.
