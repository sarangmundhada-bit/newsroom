Health tech
Normal day
The physician-in-the-loop debate went mainstream — Emanuel and the Khoslas argued in JAMA that autonomous AI can beat doctor-supervised AI on five clinical tasks, while Oracle shipped more agentic EHR capability and Orange Health's FY26 numbers landed.
Top 3
1

JAMA piece by Emanuel + Khoslas: take doctors out of the AI loop

A JAMA perspective by Zeke Emanuel, Vinod Khosla and Neal Khosla argues autonomous AI could outperform physician-assisted AI on five tasks: retrieving medical information, differential diagnosis, test selection, guideline-based prescribing, and chronic disease management. Second Opinion covered the medical-community backlash on Aug 19.
⚡ For you: Use this as ammunition for HIH's autonomy ladder — the Overton window on doctor-free AI flows (test selection, chronic management) just moved, and those two tasks map directly to 1mg diagnostics recommendations and chronic pharmacy refills. Pressure-test which HIH flows genuinely need a doctor in the loop versus which keep one for regulatory cover.

2

Orange Health FY26: Rs 139 Cr revenue, Rs 97 Cr loss, Series C prep

YC-backed at-home diagnostics player Orange Health grew operating revenue 65% YoY to Rs 138.6 Cr in FY26 but lost Rs 97 Cr before tax (EBITDA margin -67%), spending Rs 1.73 per rupee earned; it added 60+ collection centers (~50 in Bengaluru) and is down to Rs 4.5 Cr cash while prepping a $30M Series C.
⚡ For you: Direct competitive intel for 1mg Labs — Orange is buying growth in Bengaluru at brutal unit economics and is nearly out of cash. Benchmark 1mg's at-home collection cost structure against this and decide whether to press the advantage in Bengaluru before their Series C lands.

Oracle Health adds coding, chart review, dictation to clinical agent

Top 3
1

CPTO playbook: 'high AI adoption scores are meaningless'

Mind the Product's Aug 19 podcast has Tiama Hanson-Drury (CPTO, Opus 2) on leading product teams through AI adoption: ignore adoption-rate vanity metrics and look at PR size and review burden; redesign workflows from the outcome backwards instead of automating steps; deliberately keep humans in the loop where customers want it.
⚡ For you: This is your 50%-evangelism job in one episode. Steal the framing for 1mg: stop reporting AI tool adoption %, start reporting quality signals — and position human-in-the-loop as a deliberate competitive choice for health decisions, not a limitation.

2

'Extensible Software in the Age of LLMs' — users speak features

HN front-page essay arguing LLMs collapse the cost of custom extensions: ship a stable core plus safe sandboxed hooks (V8 isolates, microVMs), and let non-developers generate their own functionality via natural language — a pattern already working locally but underexploited on the web.
⚡ For you: Test this against HIH: instead of you spec'ing every insight widget, could power users (or 1mg's own care teams) prompt custom views over health data inside a sandboxed layer? That's a 10x framing for the Insights surface versus shipping widgets one PRD at a time.
