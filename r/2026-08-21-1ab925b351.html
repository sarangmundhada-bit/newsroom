Top 3
1

First mRNA cancer vaccine clears phase 3 (Moderna/Merck melanoma)

Intismeran autogene + Keytruda met both primary (recurrence-free survival) and secondary endpoints in 1,137 resected stage IIB–IV melanoma patients — the first positive phase 3 for any individualized neoantigen/mRNA cancer therapy; regulatory filings are planned.
⚡ For you: Individualized therapy makes oncology diagnostics and recurrence monitoring the growth surface — every patient on such a therapy needs longitudinal testing. Map which oncology-survivor panels 1mg Labs could own before this wave reaches India.
